# Record Structured Deliberation

Help a decision owner understand the available options, reasons, disagreements, and next action.

## Method

1. Define the question, scope, decision owner, participants, constraints, deadline, and audience for the result.
2. Collect initial positions independently when early anchoring or hierarchy could narrow the option set.
3. Normalize each proposal into its claim, assumptions, supporting reasons, tradeoffs, and open questions.
4. Link objections and rebuttals to the proposal or reason they address.
5. Run one focused challenge round on the claims most likely to change the decision.
6. Invite participants to update or retain their positions with reasons.
7. Summarize agreements, remaining disagreements, discriminating information, and available actions.
8. Hand selection to the named decision owner and record commitments with owners.

## Record

Use the team's existing meeting note, issue, decision record, or ADR format. Include:

- question, scope, owner, participants, and constraints;
- proposals and their strongest supporting reasons;
- material objections, responses, and assumptions;
- information used and important unknowns;
- position changes and stable dissent;
- decision or next decision action; and
- commitments, owners, and due conditions.

Store sensitive participant views and source material in their authorized location. Publish the minimum useful synthesis for the intended audience.

Use `analyze-critically` for a single-analyst challenge and `review-domain` for subject-specific assessment criteria.
