# Product Finish

Turn one already defined, integrated product into a coherent, deeply resolved,
release-grade experience. Keep the public story and primary interaction simple;
resolve the details behind them to high fidelity.

## Atomic boundary

Own one **Product Finish Pass** over an exact integrated product or release
candidate. The artifact records the finish target, coverage, implemented
corrections, rendered or playthrough evidence, and remaining blockers.

This Skill owns whole-product **finish** work after the product promise and
selected capabilities are defined: one finish pass / cycle over an integrated
subject. When a normal agent decides “this needs finishing / deburring,” load
**this Skill only**—there is no second finisher package.

It does not invent product strategy, business truth, prices, policy, brand
identity, game economy authority, or launch approval. It does not replace a
bounded `interface-craft` job, role cycles for prototype/build/maintain/expand,
or mixed betterment via `continuous-product-quality`.

## Finish invariant

Simple and detailed are different dimensions:

- **Simple surface** — few concepts, a legible story, obvious primary path,
  restrained controls, and coherent visual or gameplay language.
- **Deep resolution** — every material exposed detail is intentional, derived
  from an authority, explicitly not applicable, or recorded as a defect.

Depth is semantic resolution, not more prose, ornament, layers, files, screens,
mechanics, or settings. Add detail while it measurably improves meaning,
identity, comprehension, behavior, feedback, trust, accessibility, performance,
delight, or another selected outcome. Hidden trivia with no observable or
outcome-changing consequence does not need exhaustive treatment.

Functional, compilable, or clickable is not finished. A prototype, graybox,
placeholder asset, generic generated copy, arbitrary number, or low-fidelity
surface remains non-terminal unless that exact fidelity is the declared target.

## Resolve mode and target

- **Direction** — define the finish thesis and final-fidelity reference slice;
  do not edit unless implementation is requested.
- **Finish** — inspect and directly improve the integrated product, then
  recapture and verify it. A report-only response is incomplete.
- **Audit** — remain read-only and return exact, prioritized finish findings.

Declare the target state:

1. `prototype` — validates structure or mechanics; placeholders are named.
2. `final-fidelity-reference` — one representative slice proves the intended
   visual, content, interaction, sensory, and performance bar.
3. `product-candidate` — all selected surfaces meet the reference bar and
   applicable quality floors.
4. `release-finish` — the exact candidate has complete real-content,
   cross-platform, edge-state, and delivery evidence required by its boundary.

Do not silently lower the target because existing work is rough.

## Method

1. **Bind the subject.** Identify the exact source, build, content, config,
   platform, locale, route or level inventory, declared finish target, product
   promise, approved direction, user or player, and non-goals.
2. **Inspect reality.** Run or render the actual product. Traverse representative
   happy, empty, loading, error, recovery, destructive, offline or degraded,
   compact, wide, input, locale, and quality-tier states as applicable. Source
   inspection alone is not visual, interaction, or gameplay evidence.
3. **Inventory exposed detail.** Map every selected surface, journey, content
   family, asset family, mechanic, and number to the applicable finish
   dimensions. Mark each `resolved`, `not-applicable-with-reason`, or `defect`.
4. **Establish a reference slice.** If no approved final-fidelity slice exists,
   finish one representative end-to-end path first. Use it to prove the design
   language, asset quality, interaction, copy, sensory feedback, performance,
   and feasibility before repeating it across the product.
5. **Finish by coherent passes.** Correct language and numbers; visual or art
   direction; content and asset fidelity; interaction and all states; motion,
   audio, haptics or game feel; accessibility, localization, performance, and
   resilience; then cross-surface consistency. Fix owning causes rather than
   masking each symptom.
6. **Compare and repeat.** Recapture the same evidence matrix after changes.
   Compare against the reference slice, product promise, platform conventions,
   realistic content, and declared budgets. Continue while a material exposed
   defect remains and the next correction is positive-net.
7. **Deliver honestly.** Validate the exact changed candidate through its
   repository delivery boundary. Separate local, candidate, landed, released,
   and live claims. Hand launch admission to `launch-readiness-review` when a
   go/no-go decision is required.

## Product Finish Pass

Return:

- exact product/candidate, mode, target state, audiences, platforms, locales,
  input modes, and non-goals;
- one-sentence product story and finish thesis, with approved reference slice
  and anti-directions;
- surface-by-dimension coverage with `resolved | not-applicable | defect` and
  exact evidence;
- implemented corrections grouped by owning cause, including copy, assets,
  interactions, states, mechanics, and numbers changed;
- before/after rendered captures, playthroughs, measurements, or other direct
  evidence appropriate to the product;
- accessibility, localization, performance, resilience, and quality-tier
  results where applicable;
- blockers and residuals with consequence, owner, and exact closure evidence;
  and
- local, candidate, landed, released, and live delivery state without
  overclaim.

## Guardrails

- Do not polish only the hero screen while leaving the core product rough.
- Do not equate more visual effects, copy, mechanics, controls, or content with
  higher fidelity. Restraint may be the correct finish.
- Do not call generic AI copy, repeated assets, default component styling,
  graybox geometry, stock motion, or arbitrary tuning intentional without an
  approved product reason.
- Do not change a consequential number without its semantic owner, unit,
  bounds, precision, format, consequence, and validation or tuning basis.
- Do not trade away correctness, accessibility, localization, performance,
  responsiveness, input parity, recovery, or product truth for aesthetics.
- Do not fabricate claims, prices, testimonials, legal text, player findings,
  analytics, user delight, or production proof.
- Do not turn subjective prose, screenshot counts, keyword checks, or a global
  polish score into universal merge-blocking CI.
- Do not expand into unrelated capabilities or speculative decoration. Finish
  the selected product completely at its declared target.

## Completion

The pass completes only when every applicable selected surface reaches the
declared target, no material exposed detail remains accidental or placeholder,
cross-surface identity and semantics agree, the real product has been rendered
or played through at the applicable matrix, exact regressions and budgets pass,
and delivery state is reported truthfully. “No further imaginable improvement”
is not required; no remaining positive-net material defect at this target is.
