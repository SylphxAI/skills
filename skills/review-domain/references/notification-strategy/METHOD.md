# Notification Strategy

Assess whether notifications create user-valued interruption rather than
engagement hostage.

## Method

1. Name the user job, channel, urgency, consent basis, and the outcome a
   successful notification enables.
2. Open [notification strategy patterns](notification-strategy-patterns.md) for
   cadence, quiet hours, preference, and suppression design.
3. Every notification needs a stop condition, a cooldown, and a way to make
   the same outcome available in-product without the ping.
4. Transactional, account-security, and marketing traffic stay separate
   authority. A streak or campaign does not inherit emergency rights.
5. Measure useful action after open, unsubscribe, mute, and complaint — not
   send volume.

## Output

Notification findings: unjustified sends, missing consent or suppression, and
owner actions.
