# Marketplace Trust Operations

Assess whether two-sided policy, evidence, queues, enforcement, and appeals
protect participants without making one opaque score the authority for money,
visibility, or account survival.

## Method

1. Name the market, participant types, harm model, and which actions actually
   move money, visibility, or account standing.
2. Open [policy, enforcement, and appeals](policy-enforcement-appeals.md) for
   notice, ladder, restoration, and reviewer quality.
3. Open [risk queues and trust economics](risk-queues-and-trust-economics.md)
   when scores, thresholds, incentives, or fairness of the queue are in scope.
4. Check that policy, evidence, decision, notice, appeal, and restoration are
   one closed loop. A risk score may rank a queue; it must not silently spend,
   hide, or delete.
5. Consume payout, refund, ranking, identity, and generic abuse truth from
   those owners rather than re-authoring them here.

## Output

Trust-operating findings: policy gaps, unfair ladders, appeal holes, and owner
actions.
