# Drive To Delivery

Local, landed, released, deployed, and live are distinct facts. Do not poll unchanged external state. Do not become the semantic owner of other people's artifacts. A plan that keeps growing is not delivery.

The repository's declared documentation contract wins. Place each durable fact in one writable home and link to it; do not start a second status dashboard. A missing heading is not a defect. A second writable destination is.

Open [documentation placement](references/documentation-placement.md) when the objective writes or places product documentation.

Use `bound-request-scope` when the objective is still mixed. Use `select-next-work` to pick from a backlog. Use `maintain-product` for live harm.
