# Sources for this gate

Open when you need the external wording. Do not turn this file into the
procedure.

- **Evidence-based software engineering (rapid, not a full SLR):** ask an
  answerable question, search primary/current authorities, appraise, apply.
  Kitchenham, Budgen, Brereton, *Evidence-Based Software Engineering and
  Systematic Reviews*. A full protocol belongs in `synthesize-evidence-brief`.
- **Disconfirm competing methods:** Heuer's Analysis of Competing Hypotheses
  is the ancestor; this catalog's executable form is `analyze-critically`.
  Mechanical ACH matrices have mixed evidence
  (https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3550) — do not treat a matrix as an oracle.
- **Chesterton's fence:** do not remove a structure until you know why it
  exists (https://fs.blog/chestertons-fence/). Knowing why still permits a
  hard-cut on the owning layer.
- **First principles vs analogy:** decompose to the basis, then rebuild.
  Copying a neighbouring system's shape is analogy. Fleet default is
  `docs/policies/PRINCIPLES.md` Depth + Correctness.
- **Agent Skills grain:** extract procedures from real tasks; do not invent a
  skill per noun. https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/best-practices
