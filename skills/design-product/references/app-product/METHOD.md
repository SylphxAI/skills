# Design App Product

Create one App Design Blueprint around a clear user promise. The blueprint
connects user journeys, product capabilities, platform behavior, quality, trust,
operations, and specialist handoffs.

## Product direction

Choose one core concept that makes the app easy to understand and valuable to
use repeatedly. Each selected capability strengthens that concept, shares its
objects and interaction model, and includes its complete user lifecycle.

Optimize for:

- fast first value and a legible primary journey;
- durable value through saved work, continuity, creation, collaboration, and
  user-controlled personalization;
- accessible, responsive, resilient behavior across selected platforms;
- truthful commerce, privacy, permissions, sharing, and account state;
- simple ownership boundaries and reusable product primitives.

## Method

1. Define the primary user, recurring job, context, current alternative,
   promised outcome, business model, lead platforms, audience modes, and scope
   boundary.
2. Write one falsifiable product promise and the observable event that marks
   first value.
3. Research relevant products by mechanism and tradeoff. Translate useful
   mechanisms into hypotheses for this app.
4. Model canonical objects, state owners, primary and alternate journeys,
   navigation, search, creation, errors, undo, recovery, settings, admin, and
   support diagnostics.
5. Storyboard cold start through useful result, saved continuity, and the
   user's next chosen action. Place identity, permissions, payment, import,
   tutorials, and profile investment where their value becomes clear.
6. Select capabilities that serve the promise. For each one, define user value,
   owner, dependencies, availability, interface placement, data lifecycle,
   accessibility, migration, failure recovery, telemetry, and operating needs.
7. Design recurring value from useful continuity, user-created data, goals,
   relationships, history, mastery, and fresh value. Give users clear controls,
   export, deletion, recovery, and exit paths.
8. Define platform behavior for each selected web, mobile, tablet, or desktop
   target: layout, input, startup, permissions, offline and sync, local data,
   notifications, updates, accessibility, localization, and representative
   device budgets.
9. Model trust-sensitive paths with their owning authority: authentication,
   account recovery, privacy, payments, refunds, sharing, collaboration, AI,
   minors, abuse, moderation, backups, migration, incidents, and shutdown.
10. Hand specialist work to its owning skill with the app decision, required
    behavior, integration boundary, acceptance checks, and repository owner.
11. Validate the primary journey and selected edge paths through prototypes,
    behavior tests, representative devices, user observation, and current
    platform or legal sources appropriate to the claim.

## Capability availability

Core utility remains available to authorized and entitled users under genuine
product conditions such as permission, role, consent, age, territory, platform
capability, safety, service health, and external authority.

Progressive disclosure manages attention while preserving entitlement. A
dormant extension point performs zero startup, SDK, permission, collection,
network, asset, background, memory, or public-endpoint work until selected for
the product.

State-changing interactions communicate pending work, confirmed results,
failure, retry, reconciliation, undo, and recovery. Money, entitlement,
sharing, deletion, and irreversible operations show confirmation from their
owning authority.

## Output

Return one **App Design Blueprint** with:

1. user, job, context, promise, business model, platforms, audience modes, and
   scope;
2. canonical objects, state owners, primary journeys, information architecture,
   first-value storyboard, and recovery behavior;
3. selected capabilities with their reasons, owners, dependencies,
   availability, integration, lifecycle, and quality requirements;
4. recurring-value, durable-ownership, commerce, trust, platform, accessibility,
   localization, performance, offline, and support decisions that apply;
5. product measures and the smallest useful validation for material
   hypotheses;
6. specialist handoffs and implementation order.

Blueprint completion means another builder can implement the selected product
and trace each specialist decision to its owner. Implementation, release, and
live behavior remain separate delivery results.
