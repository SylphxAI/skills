# App Product

Use when the product is an application whose promise is getting a recurring job
done, not play and not a multi-tenant SaaS platform.

## Unique checks

- Fast first value without asking for setup, identity, or payment before the
  value is visible.
- Durable value through saved work, continuity, and user-controlled
  personalization — with export, deletion, and exit.
- Platform behavior for each selected web, mobile, or desktop target: input,
  permissions, offline, updates, uninstall.
- A dormant extension does zero startup, SDK, permission, or network work.
