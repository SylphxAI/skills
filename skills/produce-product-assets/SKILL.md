# Produce Product Assets

Create channel-ready assets that accurately represent the product and satisfy the destination's current requirements.

## Method

1. Confirm the product, exact build or source state, audience, message, destination, asset set, locales, accessibility needs, brand rules, rights, and deadline.
2. Read the destination's current official dimensions, formats, duration, file limits, safe zones, disclosures, and content policies.
3. Plan owned source material at sufficient quality for every selected derivative.
4. Capture or create from a stable product state with deliberate scene, device, input, locale, account, data, camera, and quality settings.
5. Produce each selected channel, aspect-ratio, locale, and accessibility variant from the best source master.
6. Preserve editability and source attribution for product capture, illustration, typography, music, voice, talent, user content, and generated material.
7. Review product-claim accuracy, composition, legibility, color, sound, captions, alt text, localization, privacy, rights, and destination specifications.
8. Correct the actual output files and repeat the affected checks.
9. Hand the final files to the owner of the store listing, campaign, product surface, or distribution channel.

## Truth and ownership

- Match every depicted feature, price, award, platform, input mode, language, performance level, and availability claim to the represented product state.
- Keep personal data, secrets, internal tooling, and unreleased states within their authorized context.
- Label concepts and mockups according to their actual state.
- Let store, campaign, and distribution owners select, publish, and verify channel state.

## Output

Return the finished files plus a concise note covering source product state, destination, dimensions or duration, locale, accessibility variants, rights or attribution, and checks performed. Include editable masters when the brief calls for future derivatives.
