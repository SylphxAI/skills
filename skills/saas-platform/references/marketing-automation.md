# Marketing Automation

Design the system that connects current product truth to selected owned,
earned, paid, and lifecycle channels.

## Unique checks

- Outcome is incremental durable user value after fees, fraud, refunds,
  support, and trust loss. Followers, clicks, ROAS, and time spent are
  diagnostics.
- Claim truth, consent, age suitability, and platform policy are release
  conditions, not optimization knobs.
- Each channel has a role, a stop condition, and a measurement that can
  disprove it.
- Creative, spend, and suppression authority are named. A campaign does not
  inherit emergency or transactional notification rights.

Return a marketing blueprint with channel roles, consent, measurement, spend
authority, and specialist handoffs.
