# Review Marketplace Trust Operations

A risk score may rank a queue; it must not silently spend, hide, or delete. Policy, evidence, decision, notice, appeal, and restoration are one closed loop. An inaccessible appeal is an unaccountable taking.

Open [policy, enforcement, and appeals](references/policy-enforcement-appeals.md) for the ladder. Open [risk queues and trust economics](references/risk-queues-and-trust-economics.md) when scores or fairness of the queue are in scope.
