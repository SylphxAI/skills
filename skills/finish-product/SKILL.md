# Finish Product

Turn one defined, integrated product into a coherent, deeply resolved
experience at its declared target. Keep the public story and primary
interaction simple; resolve the details behind them to high fidelity.

## When to use

- An integrated product needs a final finish pass across its selected surfaces.
- The work includes real copy, content, assets, interaction states, mechanics,
  accessibility, localization, performance, resilience, or consistency.
- Missing product capability routes to `build-product`; live regression harm
  routes to `maintain-product`; a bounded interface routes to
  `craft-product-interface`.

## Ownership

This skill owns one **Product Finish Pass** over an exact product or release
candidate. It records the finish target, implemented corrections, exercised
product path, and material residuals.

Product strategy, business truth, prices, policy, brand identity, game economy,
launch admission, and specialist implementation remain with their semantic
owners. This skill consumes those decisions and makes the integrated product
express them consistently.

## Finish invariant

Finish one core concept deeply:

- **Simple surface** — use the fewest concepts that express the promise, with a
  legible story, obvious primary path, restrained controls, and coherent visual
  or gameplay language.
- **Deep resolution** — resolve every material exposed detail through an
  approved authority, a deliberate design choice, or a tracked defect. Include
  feedback, recovery, edge states, and real-use quality.
- **Integrated product** — each surface deepens the same concept. Retire
  obsolete concepts that the product has replaced.

Depth means semantic resolution. Add detail when it improves meaning,
identity, comprehension, behavior, feedback, trust, accessibility,
performance, delight, or another selected outcome. The declared target decides
whether a prototype, graybox, placeholder, or lower-fidelity surface is final.

## Target

Choose the requested mode:

- **Direction** — define the finish thesis and approved reference slice.
- **Finish** — inspect and directly improve the product, then run or render the
  changed path.
- **Audit** — inspect read-only and return exact prioritized findings.

Describe the target fidelity in product terms: the accepted placeholder policy,
representative reference path, required surface coverage, real-content needs,
platforms, locales, edge states, and performance expectations.

The requested target stays fixed throughout the pass.

## Method

1. **Bind the subject.** Identify the exact source, build, content, config,
   platform, locale, route or level inventory, target fidelity, product
   promise, approved direction, user or player, and scope.
2. **Inspect the product.** Run or render representative happy, empty, loading,
   error, recovery, destructive, offline or degraded, compact, wide, input,
   locale, and quality-tier states that apply to the requested boundary.
3. **Inventory exposed detail.** For each selected surface, journey, content
   family, asset family, mechanic, and consequential number, record its current
   result, owning product when relevant, and material defect.
4. **Establish a reference slice.** When an approved reference slice is
   absent, finish one representative end-to-end path first. Use it to establish
   design language, asset quality, interaction, copy, sensory feedback,
   performance, and feasibility.
5. **Correct owning causes.** Resolve language and numbers; visual or art
   direction; content and asset fidelity; interaction states; motion, audio,
   haptics, or game feel; accessibility; localization; performance;
   resilience; and cross-surface consistency as applicable.
6. **Run the result.** Exercise the changed surfaces again and compare them with
   the reference slice, product promise, platform conventions, representative
   content, and declared budgets. Continue while a material exposed defect
   remains at the requested target.
7. **Report the delivered state.** State what is local, candidate, landed,
   released, and live. Route a launch decision to
   `review-launch-readiness`.

## Acceptance conditions

- Every selected surface reaches the same declared target, including secondary
  journeys and edge states.
- Visual effects, copy, mechanics, controls, and content earn their place
  through product meaning and user value.
- Copy, assets, component styling, geometry, motion, and tuning follow an
  approved product direction.
- Consequential numbers name their semantic owner, unit, bounds, precision,
  format, effect, and validation or tuning basis.
- Correctness, accessibility, localization, performance, responsiveness, input
  parity, recovery, and product truth remain intact.
- Claims, prices, testimonials, legal text, player findings, analytics, and live
  behavior come from their current owners and observed layers.
- CI tests product behavior and compiler contracts. Visual judgment remains in
  the exercised product path and relevant product review.
- Work stays within the selected product and target.

## Output

Return one Product Finish Pass with:

- exact product or candidate, mode, target, audiences, platforms, locales,
  input modes, and scope;
- one-sentence product story, finish thesis, and reference slice;
- surface coverage with current result, owner, and material defect;
- implemented corrections grouped by owning cause;
- the actual product paths run or rendered after correction;
- relevant accessibility, localization, performance, resilience, and
  quality-tier results;
- material residuals with consequence and owner;
- local, candidate, landed, released, and live state.

## Completion

The pass completes when every applicable selected surface reaches the declared
target, material exposed details are deliberate, cross-surface identity and
semantics agree, and the finished path has been run or rendered.
