# Finish Product

Simple surface, deep resolution of an already integrated product. A live defect is not a polish pass. New capability is not finish.

Open [finish contract](references/finish-contract.md) when target fidelity needs definition. Open [game finish](references/game-finish.md) when the promise is play. Open [app and SaaS finish](references/app-saas-finish.md) for apps, sites, and desktop products. Open [sensory feedback](references/sensory-feedback.md) when sound, haptics, camera, or effects are in the finish target.

Use `build-product` to add a missing capability. Use `maintain-product` to repair current harm.
