# Finish Contract

Use this contract to turn “polish it” into an exact, bounded, evidence-bearing
product job.

## 1. Simple surface, high resolution

A strong product is not one with the most visible detail. It is one whose
complexity has been resolved into a small, clear model and whose remaining
details reinforce that model.

Evaluate two axes independently:

| Axis | Strong state | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conceptual simplicity | One accurate story, few primary objects and actions, legible causal path | jargon, feature piles, hidden modes, duplicated concepts |
| Detail resolution | Material exposed choices are intentional, coherent, owned, and verified | placeholders, defaults, arbitrary values, contradictory states, rough edges |

Do not simplify by deleting required meaning, states, feedback, or recovery. Do
not create apparent depth through ornament, extra settings, verbose copy, or
unowned mechanisms.

## 2. Finish target

Record the selected target before evaluation. Different targets have different
truthful terminals:

- `prototype`: representative behavior or composition is learnable, with every
  placeholder and unsupported claim named.
- `final-fidelity-reference`: one representative path establishes the approved
  production bar across all applicable dimensions.
- `product-candidate`: every selected surface has been brought to the reference
  bar; no hidden “we only polished the demo” denominator.
- `release-finish`: exact real content, platform, locale, edge-state, performance,
  and delivery evidence exists at the product's declared release boundary.

Changing the target is a product decision, not a way to make a rough candidate
pass.

## 3. Detail Resolution Matrix

For every selected surface, journey, asset/content family, mechanic, and
meaningful number, disposition each applicable dimension as:

```text
resolved | not-applicable-with-reason | defect
```

### Language and content

- product, feature, object, action, navigation, status, and error terminology;
- headings, labels, instructions, help, onboarding, confirmations, recovery,
  empty states, notifications, marketing copy, metadata, and legal/policy text;
- voice, reading order, specificity, truth, localization, pluralization, text
  expansion, cultural interpretation, and content provenance;
- realistic content density and variation rather than lorem ipsum, repeated
  generated filler, or only ideal-length examples.

Every word must earn its place by conveying meaning, prompting an action,
setting expectation, providing feedback, building identity, or satisfying an
applicable obligation. Concision is not permission to omit needed meaning.

### Numbers and quantitative semantics

For every displayed, stored, transmitted, scored, priced, timed, limited,
ranked, probabilistic, or tuned value, resolve:

- semantic owner and source of truth;
- meaning, unit, dimension, sign, allowed range, bounds, and zero/null behavior;
- precision, rounding, significant figures, overflow, saturation, and error;
- display format, locale, currency, timezone, relative/absolute time, and
  abbreviation policy;
- threshold, comparison, ordering, color/label mapping, and consequence;
- update cadence, latency/staleness indication, reconciliation, and recovery;
- tuning or validation basis and counter-effects where the value shapes
  behavior, economy, ranking, difficulty, safety, or commercial outcome.

An unexplained default, magic number, false precision, inconsistent unit, or
decorative metric is a defect. The finish pass may correct representation; it
must not invent business, policy, price, economy, or scientific truth.

### Visual, graphic, and art resolution

- visual thesis, shape language, composition, hierarchy, density, rhythm,
  typography, palette, contrast, iconography, imagery, materials, lighting,
  illustration, and data visualization;
- consistency without sameness; intentional focal points and quiet regions;
- asset provenance, resolution, crop, variants, loading, failure fallback, and
  quality tiers;
- product-specific identity rather than default framework appearance, generic
  gradients, arbitrary glass effects, stock icons, or repeated generated art.

### Interaction and state resolution

- discoverability, affordance, focus, keyboard/touch/pointer/controller parity,
  target sizes, shortcuts, direct manipulation, undo, history, and recovery;
- initial, hover/focus/pressed/selected, loading, pending, optimistic, committed,
  empty, partial, stale, error, retry, offline/degraded, permission, destructive,
  success, and restored states as applicable;
- truthful feedback timing and causality; no silent action or false confirmation;
- responsive/adaptive behavior, safe areas, zoom, text scaling, orientation,
  reduced motion, high contrast, assistive technology, and low-end tiers.

### Sensory, temporal, and behavioral resolution

- animation curve, duration, sequencing, interruption, continuity, and reduced
  motion;
- sound, music, voice, haptics, VFX, camera response, and feedback hierarchy
  where applicable;
- startup, transition, response, recovery, and long-running operation budgets;
- behavior under rapid input, interruption, concurrency, failure, and repeated
  use—not only the clean happy-path recording.

### Cross-product coherence

- names, objects, states, capabilities, navigation, visual roles, pricing,
  limits, permissions, support language, and account semantics agree across
  public, authenticated, admin, mobile, desktop, email, notification, docs, and
  external integration surfaces as applicable;
- the most polished surface does not conceal a contradictory or unfinished
  adjacent surface;
- adaptations preserve product identity and meaning rather than mechanically
  copying one layout everywhere.

## 4. Final-fidelity reference slice

Before scaling assets or screens, finish one representative end-to-end path
that crosses the most important product states. Include real or representative
content, final-enough assets, responsive/input variants, accessibility,
performance, and error/recovery behavior.

The slice is a comparator, not a second semantic authority. Record:

- product thesis and anti-directions;
- exact included path and why it is representative;
- approved visual/art, content, interaction, motion/sensory, and quantitative
  decisions;
- applicable budgets and platform constraints;
- evidence and unresolved choices.

If the slice exposes a wrong product decision, return it to its owner instead
of polishing the wrong behavior at scale.

## 5. Finish passes

Run coherent passes rather than random pixel edits:

1. product story, terminology, content, and numbers;
2. visual/art direction, assets, hierarchy, and composition;
3. interaction, all states, feedback, and recovery;
4. motion, audio, haptics, VFX, camera, or temporal behavior as applicable;
5. responsive/platform/input, accessibility, localization, performance, and
   resilience;
6. cross-surface consistency and removal of placeholders, duplicates, and
   accidental variation.

Correct the shared owner when one cause appears in several places. Preserve
intentional exceptions; deduplication is not uniformity.

## 6. Evidence and stop condition

Use the real product and repeatable evidence appropriate to the medium:

- same-state before/after captures;
- representative route and journey traversal;
- real-device or representative viewport/input/locale/quality-tier matrix;
- gameplay recordings and scenario playthroughs;
- accessibility, performance, layout, content, and regression checks;
- direct measurements for claimed budgets; and
- source/candidate/released/live identity where delivery is in scope.

Subjective quality is made reviewable through a declared direction, reference
slice, exact comparators, and observable consequences—not by pretending taste
is one scalar score. Independent or multimodal critique is useful when its
expected information value exceeds its cost; it is not a ritual.

Stop when the declared target is met, every applicable matrix cell is resolved,
no positive-net material exposed defect remains, and further change would be
speculative, merely different, or lower value than delivery. Preserve newly
observed future opportunities for the continuous quality loop rather than
turning one finish pass into an immortal project.
