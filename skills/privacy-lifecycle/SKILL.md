# Privacy Lifecycle

Availability is not reuse authority. A toggle without enforceable propagation is not a control. A disabled capability creates no undeclared collection, permission, network, or retention cost.

Open [data inventory](references/data-inventory-purpose-and-controls.md) or [consent and retention](references/consent-retention-and-change.md) when the map or control machine needs depth. For a specific access, export, correction, deletion, restriction, or appeal request, use `data-rights-request`.
