# Maintain Product

Repair the owning layer. Stabilize active harm with a reversible mitigation when service impact requires it.

Open [reproduction-driven repair](references/reproduction-driven-repair.md) when the cause, reproducer, or nondeterminism needs depth. Open [frontend performance](references/frontend-performance/METHOD.md) when the defect is a measured frontend path.
