# Synthesize Market Research

Synthesize current market sources into a product, positioning, pricing, or
demand decision.

## Method

1. Open the [research synthesis method](references/research-synthesis-method.md).
   For positioning, also open
   [positioning decision](references/positioning-decision.md).
2. Define the decision, target user and buyer, category, geography, time window,
   product form, and observation that could change the decision.
3. Select category leaders, challengers, niches, substitutes, and relevant
   failed or low-trust examples by an explicit comparison reason.
4. Use current primary product, pricing, distribution, company, and regulatory
   sources. Add independent customer or market sources for material claims
   where available.
5. Compare promises, users, first value, workflow, pricing/package,
   distribution, complaints, trust gaps, and defensibility at the same scope
   and date.
6. Separate direct observation, attributed report, inference, and judgment.
   Preserve contradictory segments, geographies, plans, failed entrants,
   switching costs, and selection effects.
7. Identify table stakes, category conventions, genuine differentiation, and
   unresolved assumptions.
8. Recommend an original wedge or positioning, state confidence and risks, and
   name the smallest observation that could overturn it.

## Output

Return the decision and scope, source ledger, competitor and substitute
comparison, table stakes, differentiation, counterevidence, recommendation,
confidence, risks, and disconfirming test.
