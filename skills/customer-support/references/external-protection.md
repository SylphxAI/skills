# External Protection & Fault-Free Communication Standard

Apply these boundaries to all outbound support messages, escalation closures,
in-product mail titles/bodies, and public storefront/promotion copy.

## 1. Silent-Fix-First & Zero Unsolicited Alerts

- Fix defects, missing grants, and settlement gaps silently in the backend first.
- Never notify customers who have not contacted support, never pre-confirm
  reconciliation lists, troubleshooting progress, or unverified ETAs before
  settlement is read back, and never send unsolicited post-hoc apologies or
  retrospectives.
- Remediated in-product mail uses routine distribution titles and bodies
  (never "missed grant re-issue", "system error fix", or "apology").

## 2. Zero Fault Admission & Zero Forward Promises

- Treat remedied deliveries externally as routine system fulfillment. Report
  only the verified present state without admitting script omissions, bugs,
  callback delays, audit fixes, or ongoing repairs unless legally mandated and
  approved through legal/executive leadership.
- Never make forward promises ("our team will compensate you after checking",
  "we will follow up once fixed"). Close non-eligible or settled cases neutrally
  under unified published rules without debating formulas or tiers.
  - Bad: "Our script omitted this reward earlier; we repaired it and added it back."
  - Good: "Thanks for checking in! We verified your account records and the
    relevant event reward has been delivered to your in-game mail—please log in
    again to collect it."

## 3. Third-Party Accusations & Storefront Secrecy

- On rival/third-party reports, never confirm or deny whether another account
  received excess benefits and never promise future clawbacks or audits. Focus
  strictly on unified automated rules and the caller's own experience.
- Public storefronts and offer copy never reveal internal margin ceilings,
  discount-cap formulas (such as combined-rate ceiling rules), or static
  evergreen claims; time-limited offers follow active backend schedules with
  visible live countdowns and genuine expiry.
