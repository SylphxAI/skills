# Review Game Soft Launch

Use bounded real-player exposure to learn whether the declared game works for
its target players, markets, devices, economy, and operating model. Keep the
complete product target intact while spend and exposure remain reversible.

## Method

1. Identify the exact game build, content, economy, policy, audience, age mode,
   regions, platforms, acquisition sources, monetization, observation window,
   spend limit, and decision owner.
2. Read [game soft-launch patterns](references/game-soft-launch-patterns.md)
   when cohort design, economy controls, acquisition cells, or transfer across
   markets needs depth.
3. State the product questions: comprehension, first-time experience, core and
   meta loops, mastery, progression, social liquidity, content, economy,
   monetization, performance, support, safety, accessibility, localization, and
   market transferability as applicable.
4. Define cohorts from the few dimensions needed to answer those questions,
   such as install date, build, market, device, acquisition source, age mode,
   payer state, or treatment. Keep assignment stable and preserve a baseline.
5. Choose measures and counter-signals with definitions, sources, populations,
   maturity windows, uncertainty, and an owner action for each meaningful
   result. Representative players own judgments about comprehension, culture,
   usefulness, trust, delight, and willingness to pay.
6. Read results by exact cohort and separate product defects from acquisition
   quality, creative or listing effects, novelty, live-operations cadence,
   economy tuning, pricing, and regional differences.
7. Decide the next exposure, adjustment, hold, recovery, migration,
   compensation, or withdrawal. Keep purchases, durable progress, creations,
   and relationships through normal experiment and region changes.

## Current sources

Retrieve current store test-track, regional availability, ads and IAP,
child/age, privacy and consent, reviews/community, refund, promotion, and
acquisition-platform requirements for every active market and platform. Record
those decisions in the owning product or release repository.

## Output

Return one soft-launch assessment with:

- exact build, content, economy, policy, cohorts, markets, platforms, spend,
  observation window, and owners;
- product questions, measures, counter-signals, assignment method, and observed
  results;
- defects and market, acquisition, economy, content, service, safety, support,
  accessibility, and localization findings that materially affect the decision;
- the next bounded exposure or recovery action, its spend and harm limits,
  migration and compensation behavior, and owner.

The assessment is complete when every material result maps to an owner action,
spend and exposure stay bounded, exact versions are traceable, and transfer to
other markets remains an explicit hypothesis until observed there.
