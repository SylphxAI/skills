# Case Decisions, Remedies, And Scenario Playbooks

Resolve one customer case from current product, account, payment, policy, and
incident truth. Give the customer a direct answer, a proportionate remedy, a
clear owner, and a usable next step.

## Case state

Record the minimum state needed for the decision:

- customer, account, organization, product, plan, locale, and contact channel;
- the customer's goal, reported event, expected outcome, and impact;
- affected product version, surface, time window, and reproduction context;
- relevant account, entitlement, ledger, provider, incident, and policy state;
- prior contacts, remedies, appeals, and linked systemic issue;
- privacy, security, safety, accessibility, vulnerability, legal, or retention needs;
- responsible owner, next action, update time, and close condition.

Describe each fact by its source:

- **customer account** — the customer's description of events;
- **observed product state** — current internal or product behavior within its
  source scope and freshness;
- **owner-confirmed state** — a fact from the system or policy owner that
  governs the decision;
- **working assumption** — an explicit temporary premise used for a reversible
  next step;
- **unknown** — a material fact that needs one focused question or owner lookup;
- **conflict** — sources disagree and the owning system must reconcile them.

## Customer response

Use this structure in natural language:

1. **Recognition.** Name the specific problem and its impact.
2. **Current state.** State what is confirmed and what is still being checked.
3. **Direct answer.** Answer yes, no, partial, or pending with the useful reason.
4. **Action.** Describe the completed correction or exact next safe step.
5. **Ownership and timing.** Name the dependency owner and the next update time
   supported by the current workflow.
6. **Remedy.** Explain restoration, repair, replacement, extension, fee waiver,
   refund, credit, exception, alternative, or decline plus any real limit.
7. **Choice and appeal.** Offer available choices and an appeal route for
   consequential decisions.
8. **Close.** End concisely in the customer's language and register.

## Remedy decision

Choose the remedy that restores the promised customer outcome with the lowest
new risk:

1. verified explanation or status;
2. guided correction with customer-owned state preserved;
3. restore, repair, replace, repeat, or extend the promised service;
4. waive a fee or correct a charge through the owning payment and ledger path;
5. refund or credit through the authorized provider and entitlement path;
6. bounded goodwill compensation for verified inconvenience or lost value;
7. policy exception inside an approved authority envelope;
8. specialist, legal, security, or safety decision;
9. reasoned decline with a useful alternative and appeal.

Consider:

- promise and customer expectation;
- customer impact, duration, urgency, and recoverability;
- company contribution and systemic defect;
- money, time, data, access, opportunity, and trust loss;
- prior remedies and duplicate-compensation risk;
- contract, consumer right, provider, tax, ledger, entitlement, and policy;
- abuse evidence and false-positive cost;
- consistency across similarly situated customers;
- remedy authority, cap, expiry, and approval owner.

Money and entitlement remain separate authoritative states. The payment owner
records charge, refund, credit, settlement, and tax effects; the entitlement
owner records access changes. Support explains both.

## Scenario playbooks

| Scenario | Decision owner and inputs | Customer handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small product issue | Product owner; impact, duration, current workaround, repeat pattern | Correct or route the issue, acknowledge impact, apply a proportionate approved credit or extension when warranted, and link the systemic defect |
| Outage or widespread failure | Incident owner; affected cohort, time, plan promise, observed recovery | Use one incident source, send proactive updates, and apply an idempotent cohort remedy when warranted |
| Duplicate or incorrect charge | Payment provider and ledger owner; invoice and tax state | Correct through the payment path and confirm money and entitlement outcomes separately |
| Refund request | Refund owner; purchase, provider, applicable right or policy, usage, reason | Explain eligibility, execute the authorized provider route, preserve unrelated data, and state the entitlement effect |
| Suspected refund or chargeback abuse | Payment/risk owner; confirmed timeline, linked identities, prior pattern, consumption or transfer, dispute stage | Preserve evidence, apply proportionate commerce controls, provide notice, review, appeal, and correction |
| Trial request | Commercial owner; published eligibility, prior trials, product fit, cost/capacity, risk, sales motion | Apply deterministic eligibility or offer a demo, sandbox, limited proof, or paid pilot; state conversion and expiry clearly |
| Gift or credit request | Support/commercial owner; harm, promise, goodwill policy, repeat pattern | Grant within a reasoned policy cohort or offer a useful alternative |
| Cancellation or downgrade | Subscription owner; plan, contract, renewal date, access and data policy | State renewal, access, export, and read-only effects and provide a direct cancellation path |
| Legal or regulator contact | Authorized legal/compliance owner; exact statement, jurisdiction, contract, evidence, deadlines | Acknowledge receipt, preserve relevant evidence, provide the authorized contact and next update |
| Abusive contact | Support safety owner; behavior, threat credibility, channel history, underlying service issue | Separate service issue from conduct, set a clear boundary, apply proportionate channel controls, and preserve essential lawful access |
| Accessibility or vulnerable-customer case | Accessibility/specialist owner; expressed need, task barrier, urgency, safe accommodation | Adapt channel, format, timing, and burden; provide equivalent access and specialist support |

## Legal-contact reply structure

Use a factual acknowledgment:

- confirm receipt and the issue understood;
- state the current verified product or account facts;
- identify evidence or records being preserved under policy;
- name the authorized owner and contact route;
- give the supported next-update time;
- preserve the customer's normal support and appeal options.

## Trial decision record

Record:

- requested product and intended evaluation job;
- published eligibility and prior-trial state;
- selected proof route: trial, demo, sandbox, limited proof, or paid pilot;
- included capability, limits, data treatment, support, expiry, conversion, and
  cancellation behavior;
- commercial and abuse owner;
- customer-facing decision and next step.

## Conduct and abuse boundaries

Support continues to resolve valid service, safety, accessibility, legal, and
account rights while managing contact behavior separately. Use proportionate
tools: clear boundary, slower cadence, one authorized channel, rate limit,
specialist escalation, or safety response. Record the triggering behavior,
scope, duration, owner, review condition, and appeal route.

Credible threats enter the current safety or security incident path. Fraud and
abuse decisions use confirmed account, payment, and product evidence plus a
false-positive recovery path.

## Cultural and accessibility adaptation

- Match the customer's language, formality, name order, date/time, currency,
  number, address, and reading direction.
- Use plain language and one action per step.
- Offer accessible channels and formats, extra time, relay or representative
  support, and equivalent self-service where needed.
- Explain policy and remedy in product terms that retain the same entitlement
  and appeal across locales.
- Route legal, tax, consumer-right, and regulated differences to the current
  jurisdiction owner.

## Automation, CRM, and learning

Automation can summarize, classify, retrieve owner-confirmed state, draft a
reply, calculate an approved remedy, schedule an update, and link a product
defect. The authorized owner retains consequential money, entitlement, legal,
safety, abuse, and account decisions.

Write back the case state, decision source, action, remedy, owner, update,
appeal, and linked product issue. Dedupe repeated cases by the underlying user
problem and affected state. Send confirmed product patterns to the feedback,
incident, quality, documentation, and product owners.

## Completion

A case is resolved when the customer has a direct current answer, the owning
system has completed or accepted the action, money and entitlement state agree
with their owners, any promised update has an accountable time, and the
customer has a real continuation or appeal path where applicable.
