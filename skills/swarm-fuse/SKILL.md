# Swarm and fuse

Gather independent views when they can supply evidence that changes the decision. Whoever leads the discussion facilitates it; the accountable owner decides and records material dissent. Execution has one named writer per target.

## Frame the question

State the question, boundary and observable acceptance cases. Assign distinct angles based on the problem and participants' tools/context: a complete mechanism, a disconfirming check, relevant domain/economic effects and primary-source verification. Include simplification in an existing angle where useful; it does not require another participant.

Participants read the relevant live objects or external primary sources directly and retain the object/version and UTC read time, following [prove-it](../prove-it/SKILL.md). Form initial findings before reading others' conclusions to reduce anchoring.

## Compare and decide

Contribute evidence, clarifying questions or direct answers needed to obtain it, falsifiers and simpler alternatives. Separate observations from hypotheses. Avoid attendance/status echoes; a concise synthesis or answer for a new audience can repeat the necessary point.

Check seams between the angles and use the applicable [self-challenge](../self-challenge/SKILL.md) tests. The owner chooses the simplest complete action consistent with the evidence, records dissent and remaining falsifiers, and names the writer carrying it through [deliver-and-verify](../deliver-and-verify/SKILL.md). Unanimity is unnecessary.

On a blocked path, act on the available evidence. Ten minutes without new primary evidence is a prompt to change the method or instrument, not a required wait or a reason to interrupt a progressing job. Use [unblock](../unblock/SKILL.md) for supported recovery, existing grants and temporary-repair removal conditions.
