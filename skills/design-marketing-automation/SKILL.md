# Design Marketing Automation

Design the marketing system that connects current product truth to selected
owned, earned, paid, lifecycle, partner, store, referral, and reputation
channels. The blueprint defines channel roles, creative briefs, measurement,
spend authority, operating limits, and specialist handoffs.

## Objective

Maximize incremental durable user value and retained contribution after fees,
acquisition spend, fraud, refunds, chargebacks, support, infrastructure,
creative rights, and uncertainty. Claim truth, consent, law, platform policy,
age suitability, safety, deliverability, brand, and reputation are release
conditions.

Followers, impressions, clicks, opens, installs, acquisition cost,
platform-attributed conversions, platform ROAS, gross revenue, and time spent
are diagnostics. Incremental retained value and user value are the outcome
measures.

## Ownership

The **Marketing Automation Blueprint** owns:

- audience, intent, lifecycle, consent, and eligibility models;
- selected channel roles and activation conditions;
- message hierarchy and localized creative briefs;
- bid, budget, spend, frequency, and saturation limits;
- causal measurement, attribution, economics, and countermetrics;
- pause, correction, reconciliation, recovery, and retirement behavior.

The product owner supplies product promise, claims, prices, entitlements,
offers, release availability, and support truth. Specialist owners retain:

- exact rendered product media: `produce-product-assets`;
- official social account authority, publishing, readback, replies,
  moderation, crisis, impersonation, and recovery:
  `review-social-media-operations`;
- one promotion, referral, notification strategy, public-review policy, store
  listing, or distribution release: its named specialist skill.

Marketing translates owner-approved facts for channels and sends observed
channel outcomes back to product and commercial owners.

## Channel selection and activation

Evaluate the relevant portfolio: website and landing pages, SEO/AEO, stores,
content, official social, community, creators, PR, partners and affiliates,
referrals, cross-promotion, lifecycle CRM, reputation, paid search, paid social,
display, video, and launch communications.

Select a channel when its audience mechanism, recipient value, authority,
rights, consent model, economics, measurement, and support commitment fit the
product. For each selected channel, define:

- audience job, intent, lifecycle state, locale, territory, and age mode;
- message, recipient value, channel role, frequency, and suppression;
- owner, account or portal authority, creative rights, and current policy;
- throughput, budget, spend, failure, and saturation limits;
- activation, pause, correction, recovery, and retirement conditions;
- source events, costs, conversions, refunds, fraud, support, and trust signals.

Selection, implementation, activation, and paid spend are separate decisions.
A selected adapter can remain dormant with zero tokens, tracking, audience
exports, jobs, publishing, bidding, spend, and network activity. Activation
starts within explicit account, claim, consent, budget, quality, trust, and
safety authority. Paused channels retain the minimum readback and
reconciliation access needed to settle committed external state.

## Operating method

1. **Bind product truth.** Consume named current sources for product promise,
   proof-backed claims, price, offer, entitlement, release availability,
   territory, and support. Public copy leads with the product promise and
   category.
2. **Define the audience model.** Record job, intent, lifecycle state,
   eligibility, consent, age/territory/sensitive-category treatment, expected
   recipient value, and natural frequency.
3. **Select the channel portfolio.** Give each selected channel a role,
   accountable owner, activation condition, limits, and specialist handoff.
4. **Author creative briefs.** Include message, variant hypothesis, approved
   claims, provenance, rights, locale, accessibility, product/release
   compatibility, AI or sponsorship disclosure, expiry, and acceptance
   conditions. Product Asset Production owns the rendered files.
5. **Define economics.** Join canonical exposure, cost, conversion, refund,
   chargeback, fraud, support, and retained-value data. State attribution and
   incrementality separately. Use holdouts or experiments where feasible and
   MMM or triangulation where clean randomization is impractical.
6. **Set paid-spend authority.** Bind account, budget owner, currency, daily and
   lifetime caps, product/channel/territory/campaign limits, bid policy,
   reconciliation, and emergency pause. Platform recommendations remain
   proposals until the budget owner authorizes them.
7. **Test interactions.** Cover cross-channel frequency, duplicate conversion,
   self-competition, expired claims, deep-link availability, locale, child and
   sensitive audiences, refund/chargeback, consent drift, creator rights,
   invalid traffic, tracking loss, platform rejection, and cap divergence.
8. **Operate and learn.** Observe, reconcile, diagnose, propose, validate,
   activate within bounds, read back platform and cost state, evaluate causal
   effect, scale or pause, and update the next decision.

## Trust and authorization

- User-created work, history, identity, relationships, earned value,
  portability, export, and account access follow the product's recovery and
  data-transition contract. Renewal and return messages state changes
  neutrally.
- Sharing, referrals, reputation, update education, and cross-promotion create
  value for sender and recipient.
- Public-review requests follow the current platform policy and treat every
  sentiment by the same eligibility rule. Private feedback and support remain
  available independently.
- Advertising and lifecycle delivery use current consent, age, territory,
  sensitive-category, sponsorship, creator-rights, and platform authority.
- Creative, bidding, and recommendation systems operate inside owner-controlled
  claims, consent, spend, caps, and promotion authority.
- Current official sources supply volatile API fields, quotas, asset
  specifications, review rules, and regional requirements at execution.

## Acceptance conditions

- Every selected channel has an audience reason, owner, account authority,
  creative brief, operating limits, measurement path, and support commitment.
- Claims, prices, offers, entitlements, endorsements, availability, and product
  media trace to their current semantic owner.
- Dormant channels create zero delivery and spend. Paused channels create zero
  new delivery or spend while reconciliation completes.
- Paid channels enforce independent daily, lifetime, product, channel,
  territory, and campaign caps plus a global pause.
- Consent and eligibility precede tracking and audience export.
- Causal claims use experiments, holdouts, or a stated triangulation method;
  attribution remains a descriptive input.
- Creative includes rights, provenance, accessibility, localization,
  disclosure, brand safety, deep-link health, and release compatibility.
- Routine publishing, bidding, tuning, reconciliation, pause, policy refresh,
  and recovery have an owning adapter or specialist.
- Live claims use the exact platform account, active creative, observed spend,
  destination readback, and downstream retained-value result.

## Output

Produce one **Marketing Automation Blueprint** containing:

1. product, claim, price, offer, entitlement, release, and support sources;
2. objective hierarchy and audience, intent, lifecycle, consent, and eligibility model;
3. channel selection with role, owner, authority, limits, activation state, and rationale;
4. message hierarchy and localized creative briefs with asset-production handoffs;
5. organic, content, SEO/AEO, social, community, creator, PR, partner, store,
   lifecycle, referral, cross-promotion, update, reputation, and launch plan;
6. paid-channel bid, budget, spend, cap, reconciliation, and pause matrix;
7. event, cost, identity, dedupe, attribution, experiment, incrementality, MMM,
   economics, and countermetric plan;
8. privacy, consent, age, territory, sensitive-category, fraud, brand safety,
   deliverability, complaint, refund, and support treatment;
9. dangerous-interaction tests, recovery paths, current external decisions,
   unresolved hypotheses, and next actions.

## Completion

The blueprint is executable when selected channels have complete briefs and
handoffs, dormant channels have zero operating cost, activation uses current
authority and bounded spend, observed platform and cost state reconcile, and
incremental retained value drives scale or pause decisions.
