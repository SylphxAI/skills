# Resolve a Customer Support Case

Resolve one case through a truthful outcome, authorized action, and verified
closure.

## Method

1. Freeze channel, locale, requested outcome, and whether a reply or action is
   authorized. Sending, refunding, banning, deleting, or disclosing each need
   their own authority.
2. Verify identity only as far as the requested action requires. Never ask for
   passwords, OTPs, or full payment data in this channel.
3. Build a timeline that separates customer statements, observed system state,
   and unknowns.
4. Classify harm, urgency, company contribution, abuse/safety signals, and the
   owner of the next decision.
5. Choose the least burdensome remedy that restores the promised outcome.
   Law, safety, privacy, and irreversible-ruin floors pass first. Above those
   floors, treat materially equal facts the same regardless of threat volume
   or customer quietness.
6. Do not trade refunds or gifts for review deletion, silence, or ratings.
7. Draft a specific acknowledgment, verified facts, decision, next step, and
   appeal path. Keep internal notes internal.
8. Execute pre-authorized reversible actions idempotently, or hand off with
   evidence. Close only after customer-visible readback.

Open [case decisions and playbooks](case-decisions-remedies-and-playbooks.md)
when the remedy envelope, exception, or scenario needs depth.

Product-wide refund consequence, abuse policy, and identity recovery stay with
their owners. This method applies those policies to one case.

## Output

A case record: verified facts, decision, action/readback, customer reply, and
closure or typed handoff.
