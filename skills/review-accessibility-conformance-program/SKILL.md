# Review Accessibility Conformance

Assess whether disabled users can complete the selected product tasks and what
the current results support the organization saying about conformance.

## Method

1. Define the decision: product readiness, release, procurement response,
   VPAT/ACR input, remediation, or claim review.
2. Identify the exact product version, platforms, user roles, languages,
   content, third-party surfaces, and representative end-to-end tasks.
3. Retrieve the current primary standard, official template, platform
   requirement, or supplied procurement term that governs the decision. Legal
   and contract interpretation stay with their authorized owners.
4. Select methods from
   [accessibility conformance patterns](references/accessibility-conformance-program-patterns.md).
   Combine automated checks with keyboard, screen reader, zoom and reflow,
   contrast, target size, motion, media, cognitive, and platform-native testing
   needed by the selected tasks.
5. Record each material finding by affected user task, impact, reach,
   workaround, recurrence, owning cause, correction owner, and retest result.
6. Decide product release, procurement response, exception, and public
   conformance claim separately from the results each decision actually uses.

## Output

Return:

- exact product, decision, standard or requirement source, scope, platforms,
  workflows, assistive technologies, browsers, and methods;
- observed results and material findings with affected users and tasks;
- correction and prevention owners with focused retest results;
- release, procurement, exception, and conformance-claim decisions with their
  scope and caveats.

Protect disability-related research data through consent, minimization,
controlled access, and appropriate retention.
