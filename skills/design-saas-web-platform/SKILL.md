# Design SaaS Web Platform

Design the public-acquisition-to-authenticated-service whole for a SaaS web
platform. The blueprint connects product promise, web reach, tenancy,
entitlements, billing, support, trust, and operations while each specialist
retains its own semantic authority.

## Ownership

The **SaaS Web Platform Blueprint** owns whole-platform coherence:

- public, content, authentication, product, account, billing, admin, support,
  status, developer, and trust surfaces;
- canonical product objects, tenants, identities, roles, entitlements, and
  workflows;
- first value, recurring value, durable user investment, and exit journeys;
- web reach, responsive behavior, PWA selection, accessibility, localization,
  performance, and resilience;
- integration contracts for pricing, payments, refunds, notifications,
  privacy, security, analytics, marketing, distribution, and support.

Specialist owners retain price, provider and ledger transactions, refund
enforcement, identity recovery, public policy, exact interface implementation,
listing, distribution, and channel operations. The blueprint consumes their
named artifacts and joins their user-visible behavior.

Write the blueprint in Markdown. Name owners, sources, and handoffs in prose.
Product, price, provider, entitlement, identity, policy, and runtime facts stay
in their canonical owners.

## Capability selection

Evaluate accounts, tenancy, commerce, PWA, push, collaboration, community, AI,
developer API, integrations, search, analytics, admin, and operations against
the product promise. Select a capability when it creates product value or
fulfills an applicable quality, trust, commercial, or platform requirement.

Track selected capabilities through distinct states:

- **selected**: complete target and owner defined;
- **implemented**: behavior exists in the candidate;
- **available**: permission, role, entitlement, semantic prerequisite,
  territory, age, security, service health, or liquidity conditions pass;
- **prominent**: placement matches user need and cognitive load;
- **dormant**: zero startup, network, data, permission, background, and service cost.

This separation allows complete capabilities to remain safely dormant until
their real activation conditions pass.

## Method

1. **Frame success.** Define the recurring customer job, buyer, user,
   beneficiary, first-value event, promised outcome, differentiation
   hypothesis, business model, audiences, territories, platforms, constraints,
   scope, and falsifiable success and countermetrics.
2. **Separate surfaces.** Map public marketing, content/docs, authentication,
   product workspace, account/billing, admin, support, status, developer/API,
   and legal/trust routes. Give each route an audience, indexability,
   authority, authentication, cache, data, and failure contract.
3. **Model the product.** Define canonical objects, tenants, identities, roles,
   entitlements, workflows, state owners, history, import/export, deletion,
   audit, and cross-system handoffs. Each fact has one semantic owner.
4. **Design first value.** Choose guest, demo, sample, import, or direct signup
   deliberately. Ask for setup, permissions, profile work, and notification
   consent when their value becomes clear. Specify resume, recovery,
   cross-device continuation, empty, error, and offline states.
5. **Craft the experience system.** Establish information architecture,
   responsive shell, visual hierarchy, feedback, motion, loading,
   accessibility, content design, long and RTL text, keyboard/touch/pointer
   behavior, and low-end budgets.
6. **Design web reach.** Define semantic public pages, metadata, canonical and
   locale relationships, crawl/index controls, sitemaps, truthful structured
   data, social previews, performance, resilience, and measurement. Private
   product routes stay private; deliberately public artifacts receive their
   own route and policy.
7. **Select PWA behavior.** Choose PWA capabilities when installability,
   repeated use, offline/local-first work, resumable workflows, push, sharing,
   or device integration creates value. Define capability detection,
   service-worker updates, stale caches, storage eviction, offline mutations,
   and uninstall behavior.
8. **Design identity and tenancy.** Cover signup/sign-in, verification,
   passkeys or federation where selected, MFA, sessions/devices, recovery,
   account merge/closure, organizations/workspaces, invites, roles, tenant
   isolation, audit, and enterprise SSO/SCIM when the promise or buyer requires
   them.
9. **Join pricing to access.** Define the value exchange and hand pricing to
   `price-saas-subscription`. Compose catalog, checkout, trials, promotions,
   invoices, tax, renewals, plan changes, proration, pause/cancel,
   grace/dunning, refunds/disputes, entitlement projection, reconciliation,
   and support through their owners. For metered products, define visible
   usage, units, latency, forecast, thresholds, caps, corrections, disputes,
   and invoice reconciliation. The entitlement service owns access state.
10. **Create durable retention.** Design recurring value, saved work, history,
    collaboration, integrations, mastery, and trustworthy continuity. Use
    lifecycle education, notifications, email, trials, promotions, referrals,
    and win-back when consented, explainable, suppressible, measurable, and
    tied to real user value.
11. **Design trust and operations.** Cover privacy and data lifecycle, abuse,
    security, backup/restore, support, CRM boundaries, diagnostics, admin,
    observability, capacity, queues/jobs/webhooks, incidents, migrations,
    dependency updates, status communication, exposure, rollback, recovery,
    and shutdown. Bounded automation reconciles desired and observed state for
    routine maintenance.
12. **Define acceptance.** Specify representative workload, failure,
    device/network, locale, tenant, permission, entitlement, and recovery
    cases. Name the specialist owner, dependency order, rollout controls, and
    current-state readback for each external handoff.

## Acceptance conditions

- Every selected capability traces from customer value through canonical state,
  interface, entitlement, failure, metric, owner, recovery, and shutdown.
- Public and private routes carry explicit indexing, authentication, cache,
  data, and authorization behavior.
- Accounts, tenancy, billing, webhooks, and other consequential capabilities
  include recovery, isolation, authorization, deletion/export, replay,
  support, migration, incident, and shutdown behavior.
- Pricing, tax, provider, entitlement, security, and policy facts come from
  their current owners.
- Accessibility, SEO, security, compliance, scale, and production-readiness
  claims use the observed implementation layer that supports each claim.
- PWA, collaboration, AI, community, commerce, and developer APIs enter the
  product through an explicit product-semantic reason.
- Routine operations have bounded automation, current readback, and an
  accountable recovery owner.

## Output

Produce one **SaaS Web Platform Blueprint** containing:

1. scope, product promise, buyer/user/beneficiary, first value, business model,
   audiences, platforms, assumptions, success measures, and countermetrics;
2. public, authenticated, account/billing, admin, support, status, API, and
   trust route map;
3. canonical object, tenant, identity, role, entitlement, usage, workflow,
   state-authority, data-lifecycle, and handoff model;
4. first-use, onboarding, core workflow, lifecycle, cancellation, recovery,
   and support journeys;
5. selected capabilities with owner, target, availability, dormant behavior,
   dependencies, and selection reason;
6. responsive/input, accessibility, performance, public-web/SEO, PWA,
   localization/RTL, locale, and social-preview decisions;
7. identity, pricing, billing, entitlement, notification, support/CRM,
   privacy/security, analytics, admin, and operations integration contracts;
8. workload and failure expectations, behavior tests, rollout controls,
   migration, recovery, and shutdown plan.

## Completion

The blueprint is complete when another agent can trace every selected
capability from user value through state, interface, entitlement, failure,
metric, authority, specialist owner, availability, recovery, and shutdown.
Blueprint completion describes design readiness; implementation, whole-product
finish, release, and live availability remain distinct states.
