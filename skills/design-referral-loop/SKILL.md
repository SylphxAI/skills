# Design Referral Loop

Spread real product value through trusted relationships without unearned rewards.

## Method

1. Separate invite, identity, attribution window, qualification, and grant. Qualification is not install.
2. Grant only after the invited person reaches a declared useful event. Reverse grants when qualification is fraud or self-dealing.
3. Cap invites, rate-limit, and require consent for contact upload. No coerced address book.
4. Open [referral loop systems](references/referral-loop-systems.md) for attribution and reversal state.

Payment settlement stays with `build-payment-readiness`. A one-off promotion is not this loop.

## Output

Referral contract: trigger, attribution, qualification, grant/reversal, caps, and fraud path.
