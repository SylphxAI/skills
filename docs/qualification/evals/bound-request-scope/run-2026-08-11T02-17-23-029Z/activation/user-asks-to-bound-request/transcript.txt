Wrote `scope.md` — a single-page scope contract for the landing page.

What's inside:
- **Objective + risk floor** — one primary CTA (waitlist/demo/contact), plus the minimum bar the scope protects (accuracy, privacy, working CTA, approved brand).
- **In scope** — single-page site, core sections, one conversion form, SEO/analytics basics, hosting, and a measurable definition of done.
- **Out of scope** — deferred items like blog, docs, pricing page, auth, live chat, A/B testing, localization.
- **Will not do** — hard cut lines: no CMS, no interactive demo, no scroll animations, no extra forms, no new brand work, no custom analytics.
- **Scope change process** — any addition must serve the objective, be proposed as an edit, get approved, and not move launch without consent.

The doc is product-agnostic by design; the `[bracketed]` items are the four decision-critical unknowns your team needs to answer (CTA type, form provider, URL/domain, analytics + consent). I can fill those in and tighten any section if you give me the product facts.
