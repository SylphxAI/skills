# Launch Announcement: Acme Edge

We are excited to announce that Acme Cloud's new edge storage tier, Acme Edge, will launch on 2026-09-01 in the EU and US regions.

## Pricing

- Standard storage: $0.0045 per GB-month
- Infrequent access: $0.012 per GB-month

Existing customers on the legacy plan keep their current price until 2027-03-31.

## Beta Program

The beta program closed on 2026-07-15 with 3,214 participants from 42 countries.

## Performance and Reliability

Early access users reported p95 latency of 38 ms in Frankfurt. Our uptime target for the tier is 99.95%.
