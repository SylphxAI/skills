Launch announcement (draft)

We are excited to announce that Acme Cloud's new edge storage tier, "Acme Edge", will launch on 2026-09-01 in the EU and US regions. Pricing starts at $0.0045 per GB-month for standard storage and $0.012 per GB-month for infrequent access. Existing customers on the legacy plan keep their current price until 2027-03-31. The beta program closed on 2026-07-15 with 3,214 participants from 42 countries. Our uptime target for the tier is 99.95%, and early access users reported p95 latency of 38 ms in Frankfurt. Team lead: Dr. Mei Chen. No commit date has been set for APAC availability.
